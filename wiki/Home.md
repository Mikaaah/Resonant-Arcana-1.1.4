**Current build 1.1.4 · 228 spell definitions with optional content enabled.**

Use **Mods → Resonant Arcana → Config → First-person view** for per-spell visibility. Some detailed reference tables remain explicitly labelled with their original guide version.

An Iron’s Spells ’n Spellbooks addon built around nine schools, battlefield roles, legendary spellbooks and powerful rituals.
