# Cataclysms

**Guide version 1.1.4.** Values below come from the packaged 1.1.4 catalogue, before equipment, resistance and server settings. Two health points equal one heart. Damage is per normal hit or pulse; descriptions explain multi-stage damage. Travel and release sequences follow the cast bar.

### DOOM

Open an elevated black hole that draws visible enemies inward, then collapses for one heavy area hit. Allies and terrain are protected.

**Ender** · Cast **6s** · Cooldown **120s** · Range **32 blocks** · Radius / half-width **12 blocks**.

| Rank | Base damage | Base healing | Mana |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| I | 256 | — | 500 |

Base release / active phase: **4.5s**.

Spell ID: `resonant_arcana:doom`. Inspect ranks with `/resonantarcana spell doom <rank>`.
