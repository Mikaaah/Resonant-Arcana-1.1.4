# Spell presentation — 1.1.4

Individual spell visibility is controlled through **Mods → Resonant Arcana → Config → First-person view** or `config/resonantarcana-spell-visibility.toml`. All 228 spells have a true/false entry. See [Configuration](Configuration).

- true clears nearby first-person particles from the aim; false retains full particles.
- Settings save and apply immediately, including to existing tracked particles.
- Distant and third-person effects remain visible; damage and targeting are unchanged.
- Lightning starts with clear view enabled. Other spells retain full effects by default.

The 1.1.2 fixes restore non-lightning spell particles and raise beam/projectile sources above one-block cover. The 1.1.3 changes make charge poses hold until release, add timed God spell impacts, remove the dragon wings and Tremorzilla head, cast the Tremorzilla beam from the hands, and give Primordial Sovereign a volcanic eruption.

The 1.1.4 update preserves the 1.1.3 visual, sound and animation resources. Bosses’Rise and Alex’s Caves remain optional. Install matching Resonant Arcana versions on client and server.
