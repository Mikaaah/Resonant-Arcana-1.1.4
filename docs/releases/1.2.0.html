RESONANT ARCANA — 1.2.0

Call down a meteor. Unleash an elemental burst.

Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge · Java 21

God and Cataclysm damage

  • Reduced direct damage by 20% across all 43 existing offensive God and Cataclysm spells, including optional cave and boss spells.
  • Healing, mana costs, cast times, cooldowns and spell sequences retain their existing values.

Five Genshin-inspired Cataclysm bursts

  • Zhongli — Planet Befall: bring down a giant amber stone meteor for heavy area damage. Enemies caught in the impact are Petrified for four seconds, preventing movement, attacks and spellcasting.
  • Qiqi — Adeptus Art: Preserver of Fortune: a Cryo burst heals nearby allies and marks enemies with Fortune Talismans for 15 seconds. Allied direct hits on marked enemies heal their attacker, limited to once per second.
  • Raiden — Secret Art: Musou Shinsetsu: a sweeping violet sword strike followed by four lightning cuts.
  • Venti — Wind’s Grand Ode: an emerald vortex deals eight wind pulses while drawing ordinary enemies into its center. Bosses resist displacement.
  • Ganyu — Celestial Shower: an overhead ice pearl rains twelve targeted icicles into the marked area, dealing local area damage and slowing enemies.

Ready to discover

  • Added individual icons, Legendary scroll recipes, casting poses and searchable character/element keywords.
  • All five bursts are included in the first-person visibility settings, bringing the catalogue to 233 spells with all optional integrations installed.
  • Meteors and ice pearls are temporary visual displays; terrain stays intact. Cover, teams and the existing PvP settings still apply.

Update both client and server to 1.2.0. Requires Iron’s Spells ’n Spellbooks for Minecraft 1.21.1. The new bursts do not require Alex’s Caves or Bosses’Rise.
