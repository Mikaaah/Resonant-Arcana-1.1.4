RESONANT ARCANA — 1.1.4

Your spells. Your view.

Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge · Java 21

First-person visibility, spell by spell

  • Added a separate resonantarcana-spell-visibility.toml client config with an individual true/false setting for all 228 spells, including optional integration spells.
  • Open Mods → Resonant Arcana → Config → First-person view to change each setting in-game. Includes search, pages and reset defaults.
  • true clears nearby spell particles from your aim; false keeps the full effects. Changes save and apply immediately.
  • Particles retain their spell identity, so changing one spell does not alter another. Existing particles also respond when you enable clear view.
  • Lightning spells keep clear view enabled by default. Other spells default to full effects. Distant effects and third-person particles remain visible.

Includes the previous visibility, cast-animation, God spell sound and visual fixes. Update both client and server to 1.1.4. Each player's visibility settings remain local.
